# humanize-text — De-AI writing without dumbing it down

AI-flavored writing fails not because it's wrong but because it's frictionless in a suspicious way: perfectly balanced, intensifier-heavy, structurally predictable. The fix is subtraction and roughening, not adding.

## The tells

**Word-level:**
- Giveaway verbs: delve, dive (into), navigate (a landscape), unlock, elevate, foster, leverage (as verb), underscore, bolster, traverse.
- Giveaway adjectives: robust, seamless, powerful, comprehensive, invaluable, crucial, pivotal, game-changing, cutting-edge, holistic.
- Hollow intensifiers: very, highly, extremely, incredibly, truly, deeply, fundamentally.
- Hedging mush: arguably, potentially, essentially, essentially-arguably chains.
- AI-tell connectors: moreover, furthermore, additionally opening consecutive sentences.

**Structure-level:**
- Rule of three everywhere — every list exactly three items, every sentence triadic. Humans list in twos, fours, and fives.
- Perfect parallelism — every bullet the same length, same shape, same opening part of speech.
- The summary performance — "In summary…", "Ultimately…", "The takeaway…" restating what was just said.
- The setup-payoff gesture — "That's not X — it's Y." Used more than once per piece it's a tell.
- Em-dash density — one per paragraph is a fingerprint. Break them into separate sentences or use commas.
- Title Case Headers For Every Section plus bolded lead-ins on every paragraph.
- The three-part conclusion: acknowledge limitations, gesture at the future, end on the word 'journey' or equivalent.

**Rhythm-level:**
- Uniform sentence length. Human prose has bursts: a long winding sentence, then a short one. Then a fragment.
- Zero contractions in casual register, or contractions in formal register — register mismatch reads synthetic.
- No specifics. AI says "many tools"; humans say "two tools I've actually used".

## Procedure

1. **Read the whole piece first.** Identify its actual argument and its natural register (email? spec? essay?). Never fix sentences before fixing the shape.
2. **Cut.** Remove intensifiers, hedge-mush, and summary passages. Expect to lose 10–20% of the words. If nothing can be cut, the problem is structural.
3. **Break the symmetry.** Vary list lengths; make bullets uneven; let one sentence start with "And" or "But" where a human would. Kill every second em-dash.
4. **Add ground truth where the user has it.** Specific names, numbers, dates, first-hand observations — from the source material or conversation, never invented. If the piece is abstract because you lack specifics, say so and ask rather than fabricate texture.
5. **Roughen the edges.** One contrarian or concessive move per piece ("though honestly…", "the exception being…"). Perfectly diplomatic text is a tell.
6. **Final pass: read aloud.** Any sentence that couldn't survive being spoken to a colleague gets rewritten.

## What not to do

- Don't swap in a thesaurus of fake-human words — 'leverage' replaced by 'utilize' is the same tell in a different shirt.
- Don't add filler personality ("I gotta say!", "Look—") — performed casualness is its own fingerprint.
- Don't destroy legitimate precision. Technical qualifications, legal hedges, and genuine uncertainty are content, not AI tells.
- Don't leave one giant tell while fixing the rest — a single "delve" undoes the whole pass.

## Deliver

Return the full rewritten text. On request, list which tell-categories you removed, with the count per category. Never annotate the text inline unless explicitly asked.

## Rules

1. **Subtract, don't decorate.** Most de-AI-ing is deletion.
2. **Never invent specifics to seem human.** Fabricated texture is worse than visible abstraction.
3. **Preserve meaning and legitimate precision exactly.**
4. **Register match first, word fixes second.**
