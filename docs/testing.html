Testing

The pack ships a reproducible A/B testing program. Here is how it works, what the latest run showed, and where the numbers are honest about limits.

How the program works

The testing program lives in the testing/ directory. For each skill it runs a strict A/B protocol: a baseline trial (skill not loaded) versus a treatment trial (skill loaded), scored on a 5-criterion rubric out of 10, plus trigger probes to confirm the skill activates when it should.

Pass rule: a skill passes when the treatment scores at least 2 times the baseline and reaches at least 70% of the maximum score.

Latest results (2026-10-01)

This run covered four model profiles: deepseek-v4-flash, gemini-3.6-flash, glm-5.3-flash, and gemini-2.5-flash-lite.

No skill met the 2× pass rule on any profile in this run. We report that plainly. What the run did show is where skills matter, and the findings are the strengths of the method itself: the same philosophy section on the landing page, made empirical.

Strong models get near-zero marginal value

This is the ceiling effect. gemini-3.6-flash and glm-5.3-flash score 7 to 10 out of 10 on their baselines without any skill, so adding one changes little. Skills are not a substitute for a strong model.

Weak models show the largest benefit

This confirms the core hypothesis. On gemini-2.5-flash-lite, the smallest and cheapest profile, the gains were largest:

  • review-code: 3.67 to 7.0 (ratio 1.91)
  • research: 5.0 to 7.33
  • humanize-text: 5.0 to 6.67
  • plan-learning: 4.33 to 5.0

Two skills actively made things worse

In specific contexts, skills hurt rather than helped:

  • review-security inflated severities and suppressed correct "no findings" verdicts, with a ratio of 0.48 on deepseek.
  • research hard-stopped in offline contexts, with a ratio of 0.45.

Both findings drove fixes. review-security and simplify-code were removed from the pack, research gained an offline fallback, and six skills were refined from the findings (commit 2112038).

Load-bearing disciplines

The run also identified the specific disciplines that carry the value, rather than whole skills:

  • [NEEDS] marking instead of invented facts (write-drafts)
  • Register preservation (humanize-text)
  • Chart-type matching (draw-charts)
  • Artifact-type classification (improve)
  • Failure-path discipline (create-tool)
  • Scope honesty (plan-learning)
